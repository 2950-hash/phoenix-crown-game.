🌋 THE LEGEND OF EMBERIA 🌋

Once, the kingdom of Emberia flourished under the reign of mighty Fire Phoenix Kings— guardians who kept fire, water, earth, and air in eternal harmony. For centuries, peace reigned. Then darkness came.

The wicked Abyssal Fish King, ruler of the Water Kingdom, stole the sacred Elemental Crown and unleashed devastating floods that shattered Emberia's throne. The Fire Kingdom fell to ruin. The crown shattered into lost fragments, scattered across the world.

You are Pyro — a young Fire Phoenix with blazing wings and an unbreakable spirit. Travel through the Ashwood Forest, the Scorched Mountains, the Frozen Ocean, and the Sky of Storms. Defeat mighty bosses, reclaim the Crown Shards, and challenge the Fish King in his underwater fortress — the dreaded Abyssal Palace.

Rise from a single spark. Become the Fire King. 🔥👑🦅
